Privacy Policy

Data & Privacy Protection

Effective Date: October 7, 2026 • Application: Chromium

1. Information We Collect

Chromium collects only the minimal Discord platform data necessary to process user commands and maintain system security:

Discord User IDs: Used to associate command permissions, rate limits, and queued requests with specific users.
Guild (Server) & Channel IDs: Used to route responses, track server-specific configurations, and deliver output to the correct channel.
Command Logs: Transient execution logs containing error codes and timestamps to identify bugs and mitigate spam.

2. How Data Is Used & Shared

Collected data is used exclusively to operate Chromium, enforce safety limits, and fix technical bugs. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your data with any third parties under any circumstances.

3. Data Retention & Erasure

Temporary command logs are automatically purged every 30 days. You may request immediate deletion of any configuration data associated with your User ID or Server ID by submitting a removal request.

4. Data Deletion Requests

If you wish to request data removal or have questions regarding this policy, contact us at support@yourdomain.com or open a ticket in our support server.
